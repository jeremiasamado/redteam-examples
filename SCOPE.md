# Scope and safety

This repository contains self-authored samples designed for local analysis.

- Use the material only on systems and software you own or are explicitly
  authorized to assess.
- Do not repurpose the lab tooling against third-party software or services.
- Do not commit credentials, private data, live targets or weaponized payloads.
- The sample's token and data are synthetic and have no external value.

The purpose is to demonstrate analysis, reasoning and reporting — not to bypass
commercial licensing or access real systems.
